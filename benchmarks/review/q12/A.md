# Briefing A: Should we build a sodium-ion battery cell plant in Morocco to supply European carmakers?

## Situation

Defer Moroccan plant capital for 12 months; license sodium-ion technology and start OEM sampling first. Gotion started commissioning an LFP cell plant in Kenitra the week of 20 October 2026 [1]. Reports put its first phase at 10 or 20 GWh a year. CATL unveiled its Naxtra sodium-ion cell with Changan on 5 February 2026 [2]. CATL claims up to 175 Wh/kg. One 2026 analysis expects the sodium-ion cost crossover with LFP in 2028–2029 [3]. Other sources conflict on current cost. Searches found no European OEM sodium-ion offtake agreements. The Commission's Battery Booster call of 28 July 2026 covers only projects in the European Economic Area [4]. COM(2026) 100 of 4 March 2026 promotes Made-in-EU requirements for battery cells [5]. France and Germany reportedly favour a restrictive Made-in-Europe definition [6]. Battery Regulation due diligence applies from 18 August 2027 [7]. Renewables supplied about 37–40% of Morocco's 2023 electricity [8].

## Complication

Gotion began commissioning an LFP cell gigafactory in Kenitra in October 2026 [1]. A Chinese competitor now occupies the Moroccan site a sodium-ion entrant would target. The Industrial Accelerator Act proposal requires battery cells to be produced in the EU [2]. The act is not law yet [3], and the Council remains split. Battery Booster loans are open only to projects located in the EEA [4]. A Moroccan plant is therefore outside the €1.5bn interest-free loan facility. No European OEM offtake agreement or sodium-ion model announcement has been found. Sodium-ion still carries a cost premium to LFP in one 2026 analysis. That analysis expects the cost crossover in 2028–2029 [5], not earlier. Sources conflict, and none is primary. CATL already has a sodium-ion car in mass production [6] with Changan. Committing factory capital before these facts settle risks a stranded asset. The tension is sharp: waiting cedes ground, and building early risks stranded capital.

## Key Question

Should the requester fund a Moroccan sodium-ion cell plant before EU origin rules are final?

## Answer

The requester should hold Moroccan factory capital until EU origin rules are final. Within 12 months, it should license a cell design, obtain toll-made samples and sign named buyers. That avoids Gotion-scale exposure, reported at $1.3bn for phase one.

No European OEM sodium-ion demand is documented. EEA-only Battery Booster loans and split Council positions leave Moroccan origin status open. Choose Morocco or Iberia only after a sourced landed-cost benchmark exists.

## Supporting Arguments

European demand and cost evidence do not yet justify factory capital before 2030. Research found no European OEM offtake agreements, letters of intent or sodium-ion model announcements. Analysts now expect sodium-ion's cost crossover with LFP in 2028–2029 [1]. Sodium-ion has reached Chinese roads but not yet Europe [2]. A licence and toll-made samples can start OEM qualification now.

Regulatory rules disadvantage Moroccan cells until the Industrial Accelerator Act is final. The proposal states that battery cells must be produced in the EU [3]. The Council remains split on which countries count [4]. Lawmakers are pushing back against a more restrictive line favoured by France and Germany [5]. Battery Booster loans are open only to projects located in the EEA [6]. Morocco's grid was only 37–40% renewable in 2023 [7]. EU due diligence duties apply from 18 August 2027 [8]. Defer factory capital until the final text settles origin.

Chinese incumbents already lead in Kenitra and in sodium-ion technology. Gotion began commissioning its Kenitra gigafactory in October 2026 [9]. CATL says Naxtra cells reach 175 Wh/kg in a mass-production passenger car [10]. CATL has also signed European sodium-ion storage deployment deals. Energy Live News reports 3 GWh of sodium-ion storage will be installed in Europe by 2031 [11]. A licence with yield guarantees counters this better than building first.

## So What

Chief Commercial Officer: sign a sodium-ion licence or JV, with toll-made B-samples, within 12 months. Without it, OEM qualification never starts. Volume supply slips to 2030 or later, after Gotion's 10–20 GWh Kenitra phase [1] and CATL lock in the first European contracts. Commit no factory capital until the Industrial Accelerator Act's final text treats free-trade-partner cells as Union origin. Then compare Morocco and Iberia on sourced landed-cost and cost-of-capital data. Sign named storage and OEM buyers in parallel.
