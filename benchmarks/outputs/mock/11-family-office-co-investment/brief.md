## Options and Tradeoffs

Three options are available and each is presented as a genuine alternative. The first is to authorise the co-investment at a capped envelope, conditional on advisor recusal, independent counsel second opinion, and a written statement from the successor generation on record before the vote. The fiscal exposure is bounded inside the current sleeve target; governance impact is positive because the file establishes the standard for advisor-led co-investments; generational alignment is mixed and mitigated by a review clause at succession; reputational surface is low under a documented record. The second option is to defer to a formal study led by an independent counsel and revisit at the following cycle. Fiscal exposure is nil in the current cycle; governance impact is a durable signal of a high bar on advisor-led files; generational alignment is improved by treating the successor generation's preference procedurally; reputational surface is very low. The third option is to decline this cycle and formally record the reasons. Fiscal exposure is nil; governance impact is a strong precedent that advisor-led files carry a higher bar; generational alignment is fully preserved; reputational surface is very low internally, with a small external cost in the advisor relationship. The recommended course is the first option, subject to all three dependencies being satisfied on the day of the council session; if any dependency is missing, the file defaults to the second option rather than being taken to a vote.

## Risks and Preservation Considerations

Five material risks apply to the recommended option and each is paired with a concrete preservation-oriented mitigant. The governance risk of the advisor's dual role is the highest and is discharged by a formal recusal and an independent counsel second opinion filed before the vote. The generational-alignment risk of authorising over the successor generation's documented resistance is medium and rising, and is mitigated by inviting a written statement into the record and by embedding a review clause at succession. The concentration risk of taking the direct venture sleeve above the Campden soft ceiling is discharged by capping the envelope and by scheduling a rebalancing review six months after completion. The regulatory risk of the tightening FINMA beneficial ownership expectations is discharged by preparing the filing in draft before the vote so it can be lodged within the thirty-day window. The reputational risk of a governance failing surfacing in trade press is low and is discharged by documenting the complete governance trail with the STEP mitigants applied. The reputational and financial risks are distinct throughout — the reputational surface of the file is contained by the record, and the financial surface is contained by the cap and the rebalancing schedule.
