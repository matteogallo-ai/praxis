## Situation

**Hold a full-scale Moroccan sodium-ion cell plant for 6–12 months and buy a staged option now.** Upside: Morocco near-shoring. Risk: unclear EU origin rules and cheap lithium. Recommendation: stage-gate the decision.

Morocco now has battery manufacturing capacity. Gotion Power Morocco began commissioning production equipment the week of October 20, 2026 at its battery gigafactory in Kenitra ([WINS](https://www.winssolutions.org/gotion-kenitra-battery-gigafactory-morocco/)). The plant uses lithium-iron-phosphate (LFP) battery technology with an initial capacity of 10 GWh intended for the automotive market ([pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/08/03/morocco-hosts-africas-first-battery-manufacturing-plant/)). Other sources give the first phase as 20 GWh, so reported figures differ. Gotion and PowerCo plan a Kenitra cathode project set at about €480 million and 100,000 metric tons a year ([Morocco World News](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/10/341754/gotion-to-launch-moroccos-first-battery-gigafactory-in-kenitra-the-week-of-october-20/)).

Sodium-ion cells reached cars in 2026. Since February 2026, the Changan Nevo A06 has been in production with CATL cells ([Denkstrom](https://denkstrom.org/en/goodnews/catl-sodium-ion-batteries-mass-production/)). China holds more than 95% of installed and announced SIB manufacturing capacity ([DataDeep](https://datadeep.tech/sodium-ion-batteries-2026/)).

## Complication

The EU's draft Industrial Accelerator Act (IAA) could mean a Moroccan cell no longer counts as European from 2027–28. The ACEA study says that six months after the law enters into force (around 2027–2028), batteries would need to include three EU-sourced components, including battery cells ([electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2026/09/29/industrial-accelerator-act-industry-may-fail-to-meet-battery-demand-in-eu/)). The same study calls the rules of origin unclear on whether near-shoring free trade partners like Morocco are treated as equivalent to local EU production (same source). Carmakers' trade body ACEA wants the scope narrowed to EU27 + the UK, whilst also protecting existing investments of European manufacturers in selected countries (Turkey, Morocco) in a targeted way ([ACEA](https://www.acea.auto/press-release/acea-publishes-position-on-the-industrial-accelerator-act/)). The words "existing investments" may not cover a new plant built after the rules are set.

Sodium's cost argument is also weaker now. Lithium carbonate futures briefly hit Yuan 209,800/tonne in mid-May 2026 ([Mysteel](https://www.mysteel.net/h/lithium/)). By November 4 the spot price was 86460 yuan per tonne ([SunSirs](https://www.sunsirs.com/uk/frodetail-1162.html)).

## Key Question

Should the company commit capital in the next 12 months to a full-scale sodium-ion cell plant in Morocco for European carmakers?

## Answer

No: do not commit full-scale capex within 12 months; fund a staged option that can be scaled up by 2028. The plant's value depends on two things that are not yet settled: whether the IAA treats Morocco as local, and how much cheaper sodium is than LFP. The estimated roughly 30 percent manufacturing cost advantage over LFP applies only given adequate production capacity ([Denkstrom, citing IEA](https://denkstrom.org/en/goodnews/catl-sodium-ion-batteries-mass-production/)), and a new entrant would not have that scale at launch.

## Supporting Arguments

**Through 2028, regulation is the biggest risk to the business case.** Transport & Environment notes that companies may simply set up factories in Morocco and get all the benefits ([T&E](https://www.transportenvironment.org/articles/how-the-eu-industrial-accelerator-act-can-help-avoid-more-battery-factories-going-bust)). Brussels may tighten the text in response. Under the draft, the required list grows around 2030–2031 to five EU components, including battery cells, battery management systems, and active cathode materials ([electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2026/09/29/industrial-accelerator-act-industry-may-fail-to-meet-battery-demand-in-eu/)). If Morocco falls outside the definition, the product loses its value as a "European" cell.

**Today, the economics favour scaled Chinese incumbents over a new entrant.** CATL has announced plans to build annual production capacity of 60 gigawatt-hours for sodium-ion cells by the end of 2026 ([Denkstrom](https://denkstrom.org/en/goodnews/catl-sodium-ion-batteries-mass-production/)). Its vehicle ramp-up for 2026 is still modest: 10,000 to 20,000 vehicles fitted with Naxtra batteries (same source). Cheaper lithium also shrinks sodium's raw-material advantage over LFP. Making both chemistries on one site is feasible, because sodium-ion cells can use existing manufacturing infrastructure with minor modifications ([R&D World](https://www.rdworldonline.com/175-wh-kg-how-catls-naxtra-architecture-moves-sodium-ion-beyond-the-lab/)).

**Morocco remains a sound location for a later, staged entry.** Gotion says its Kenitra output will serve automotive production sites based in Morocco and in Southern Europe ([pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/08/03/morocco-hosts-africas-first-battery-manufacturing-plant/)). The country had more than $14 billion in automotive exports in 2023 ([Ecofin](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-industry/1609-48731-as-morocco-attracts-a-5-6b-battery-plant-its-coal-fired-grid-faces-a-power-test)). The same source flags Morocco's coal dependency, which could affect cell carbon footprints under EU battery rules. European carmakers have shown interest in sodium: Stellantis invested in Tiamat to diversify its portfolio ([Reuters via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/stellantis-invest-french-sodium-ion-063312960.html)). That article is older than the 545-day freshness limit.

## So What

The strategy lead should do three things by Q2 2027:
- Seek a carmaker letter of intent for sodium-ion offtake.
- Hold talks with a technology licensor.
- Lobby to get Morocco recognised as local under the IAA.

Doing nothing risks the IAA being finalised without covering new Moroccan investment, which would strand any later plant. The euro cost of that cannot yet be quantified (not sourced).
